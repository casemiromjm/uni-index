A short record of the projects I've worked on throughout uni. Initially in [Bachelor in Informatics and Computing Engineering (L.EIC)](#bachelor-in-informatics-and-computing-engineering) and then in [Master in Informatics and Computing](#master-in-informatics-and-computing-engineering), both at the [Faculty of Engineering of the University of Porto (FEUP)](https://www.up.pt/feup/en/). Some exercises/repositories are not public for the sake of avoiding any possible trouble that may arise and due to its lower relevance.

The structure of this page is heavily inspired by a similar repository from [peucastro](https://github.com/peucastro/feup) and, by extension, [limwa](https://github.com/limwa/feup) and [bdmendes](https://github.com/bdmendes/feup).

- This is not a complete collection of all the work I have done. I have only included the work I consider to be relevant and that I am allowed to share.
- This is intended for educational and historical purposes only. The code and other artifacts presented may be outdated or incomplete.
- I am not responsible for any issue that may arise from the use of this content. If you choose to use it, you do so at risk of failing your course or getting into any other kind of trouble.

With that being said, feel free to explore the repositories or other deliverables.
